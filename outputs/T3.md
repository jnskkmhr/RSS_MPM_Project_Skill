Metric definitions currently used:

| Quantity | Definition / limit |
|---|---|
| Horizontal tracking error [m/s] | Mean norm of yaw-aligned horizontal velocity minus commanded XY velocity; not “RMSE” and not just forward velocity. Report mask is t≥1 s and root X≥bed lower X+0.2 m. |
| Action rate [rad/s] | RMS of finite differences of policy actions ×0.25 /0.02 s; commanded joint-target rate, not actual joint velocity. |
| Torque RMS [N·m] | RMS of sampled applied joint torque. Not cost of transport. |
| Success/fall | All-trial bars; tracking/action curves use successful traversals only. A method with no successes has no tracking curve there, not zero error. |
| CoM drift [m] | Mass-weighted body CoM in tray frame, horizontal distance from t=2 s reference; excludes initial settling. |
| Sole lift [m] | Ankle-roll position plus rotated `(0,0,-0.03539)` m; subtract interpolated minima of sustained force-contact segments (>5 N for ≥2 samples), clamp negative values to zero. This is estimated lift above stance level, **not measured sand-surface clearance or sinkage**. |
| Foot force [N] | World-frame rigid+MPM contact force sampled at 50 Hz; plots use Fz and norm(Fx,Fy) on the flat material bed. Not a physics-rate landing-impact peak. |

## 6. Failed attempts: what was tried and did not work, and why.

- Earlier custom testbeds were rejected by the user. They changed unauthorized geometry/test conditions. Their metrics are archived and must not be mixed with native regenerated data.
- Early narrow-bed side exits were conflated with locomotion failure. Width was increased to 4 m for slopes; outcomes now distinguish forward completion, physical falls, side/backward exits and timeouts. Side exits censor later behavior, so “not fallen yet” is not proof of eventual success.
- Earlier rigid slope configurations had an approach/ramp mismatch and foot-catching behavior. Final saved protocols use the native generated planar ramp, entrance height matching, zero margins and more frequent rigid contact updates. Old plots/videos are not validation of the final collider setup.
- Three parallel full-resolution wide MPM beds caused OOM on the local ~32 GB GPU. Rigid uses three environments; MPM retains sequential seeds. Concurrent unrelated GPU jobs also affected runtime. No throughput claim is made from these evaluation wall times.
- Old RFT rough run `05rx8mub` lacked actual rigid-support geometry in Newton: support was authored outside the imported `/World/ground` root. Moving it under `/World/ground/support` fixed import. `osmo_contact_benchmark.md` records a sphere-drop regression; earlier geometry-only checks missed the issue. This invalidates treating that old shuffling policy as a clean RFT baseline.
- Historical actor-loading mismatches included command history, quaternion order and joint/action dimensions. The benchmark loader checks actual saved architecture and maps commands. `hiwy2c7j`/`kyfj909z` have command history (1,000 actor inputs); later no-history actors have 937. These adaptations are not evidence that all reward/event/asset settings match.
- Standard `.usd`, converted `.usda`, four-sphere and box-foot assets were mixed across experiments. USDA pelvis-contour/hip-pitch collision exclusions were later added. The file extension alone is not the physics difference; exact collision relationships and geometry matter.
- Repeated reward changes (constant pitch vs stance-only pitch, pitch difference, foot separation, sliding, symmetry loss) produced different recipes. Do not attribute all gait differences to the RFT/MPM model.
- The material adaptation sweep ends early for faster traversals. It cannot support an endpoint-adaptation claim despite 100% traversal success.
