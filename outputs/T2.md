- Rigid ground: friction of the ground and of the cube 0.3, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9 and 1.0 (`--friction`; 0.5 is the task's value). Settings otherwise those of `tow_rigid_final`.

**Measures.** Per terrain: tether tension (mean from 5 s over the trials that stay up), cube speed, net distance of the cube, goals, falls, torso pitch.

**Rules fixed beforehand.**
- A terrain gives a point when at least 3 of 16 trials stay up and the cube moves at 0.1 m/s or more; terrains that give no point are marked.
- Per solver the figure shows the minimum, the maximum and the mean of the terrains' tensions, and every terrain as a point.
- The cube speed of every point is reported. Points whose cube speed is not within 0.05 m/s of 0.50 m/s are marked as not at equal speed, and the range over the equal-speed points is given beside the full range. If most RFT points are far below 0.5 m/s this is said on the slide and to the lead, because the solvers' forces are then not compared at equal speed.
- Posture insets: side views of the robot at three terrains (rigid ground, a mid sand, the softest usable sand), from renderings of single-environment runs if clean, otherwise a drawing with the measured torso pitch; labelled with the angle only.

**Where it runs.** Local RTX 5090, as the earlier rigid and RFT runs.

**Addition, fixed after the first results and before running it (2026-11-24).** On all 13 RFT sands the MPM-trained policy moves the cube at 0.12 to 0.43 m/s, so no RFT point is at equal speed. To have an RFT tension at about 0.5 m/s, the RFT-trained tow policy (`9733ux8d`, final checkpoint) is run on the same 13 RFT sands with the same settings (folders `rftpolicy_d*_f*`). These are reported beside the figure as "RFT sand towed by the RFT-trained policy" and are not mixed into the MPM-trained policy's points; whether they go into the figure is the lead's decision.
