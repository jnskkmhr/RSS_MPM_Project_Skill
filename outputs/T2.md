**Bed setting, stated as a condition.** Main setting for every sand: all particles awake, template settled for 12 s. **Deviation, noted when the first tasks ran (2026-11-24).** With the task's sleeping and 2.5 s settling, dense sand, light sand and loose poppy seed have not come to rest on the 0.6 m bed after 2.5 s (largest particle speed 0.031 to 0.035 m/s against the 0.03 m/s limit), and the run refuses to start. The settling time is a fixed duration, not an upper limit. For these three sands the sleeping setting is run with 12 s settling instead (workflow `t2-tow-sands-sleep12-1`, folders `*_sleep12`), so for them the comparison isolates sleeping; beach sand and packed poppy seed run with sleeping and 2.5 s as planned.

**Conditions per cell.** 15 kg cube attached (command held until 10 m of path or 40 s), and tether cut (fixed 20 s). Command 0.5 m/s straight. 16 trials per cell: 2 runs of 8, seeds 42 and 43, start as in training. 1.0 m/s is not run in the first pass.

**Measures.** Stayed up, goal reached, cube speed, tether tension, horizontal tether pull (equal to the horizontal ground force on the cube over a trial), cube depth below the settled surface and below its rest level, torso pitch, pelvis ahead of the stance foot, velocity error. Means from 5 s to the end of each trial, then mean ± std over the trials that stayed up; a cell needs at least 3 such trials.

**Rules fixed beforehand.**
- Tension and force are compared only between cells whose cube speeds are within 0.05 m/s of each other. Where a policy does not move the cube (cube speed below 0.1 m/s) that is stated and no force is given.
- Criterion for B: per policy, Spearman rank correlation between the cell means of tether tension and of torso pitch over the grounds where the policy stays up in at least 3 of 16 trials with the cube attached, with the number of grounds. Reported whichever way it comes out, together with the same correlation restricted to cells at equal cube speed.
- Answer to A per sand: "walks without load" means at least 14 of 16 trials up with the tether cut; "fails with the cube" means fewer than 14 of 16 goals with it.

**Where it runs.** OSMO, pool `isaac-lab-l40-07`, one single-GPU task per sand and bed setting. Slides: two figures, labelled unchecked.
