Definitions:
- Goal: the robot's path (its horizontal speed summed over time) reaches 10 m before the time limit. It is a path length, not a distance from the start. Fall: any termination, which is a pelvis tilt above 45 degrees or the pelvis-height term.
- Errors: mean absolute difference between the base-frame velocity and the command over each trial from 5 s to its end, then mean ± std over the trials without a fall. `vy` is always commanded as 0; the tow training commands only `vx` (0 to 1 m/s) and `wz` (within 0.5 rad/s), so a `vy` command is outside training and was not tested.
- The base frame leans forward with the pelvis (about 20 degrees while towing), so the robot's horizontal speed in the world frame is given too. At the 1.0 m/s command on this bed the robot tows at 0.69 m/s: it does not reach the commanded speed with the 15 kg cube.
- The `wz` error includes the yaw oscillation of each step; the mean yaw rate is beside it.
- With 0.5 m/s and 0.5 rad/s the robot walks a circle of 1 m radius, shorter than the 1.2 m tether, so the cube stays near the centre and pivots (cube speed 0.01 m/s). That row shows turning while tied to the cube. The 1.0 m/s turning row drags the cube along a curve (cube speed 0.43 m/s).
- Bed depth: the policy was trained on a 0.25 m bed, where its feet come within 2 cm of the rigid base in 16 of 16 towing trials. It is evaluated on a 0.45 m bed, where the lowest sole corner stays 8 cm or more above the base. On the 0.25 m bed the same checkpoint reaches the goal in 16 of 16 at both straight commands, with the cube at 0.50 and 0.92 m/s (`logs/t2/tow_d25`); the bed-depth table is in `outputs/T3.md`, block T2-TOW.
- Training setup of this policy: half of the environments on rigid ground and half on MPM sand; the cube mass on sand follows a curriculum from 5 to 15 kg; forward lean up to 20 degrees is not penalized; one fixed sand material, no material randomization.

| Property | Value |
