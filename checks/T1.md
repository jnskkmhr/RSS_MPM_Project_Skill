## Issues
Each with what is wrong and what is needed to fix it.

1. **Two P2 runs shared the GPU with another process.** `envs_full_sleep_on_n1_profile.json`: `after_create_device_MiB` 11,832 against `after_create_process_MiB` 4,202, peak device 18,596 against process 8,976, so 7.6 to 9.6 GB belonged to something else; its first 200 steps are about twice as slow as the plain run (284 / 241 / 197 / 158 ms per 50-step block against 140 / 115 / 88 / 70). The reported "physics per env 124.42 ms" for full, sleep on, 1 env comes from this run and is wrong (it exceeds that row's own step time of 77.49 ms). `envs_full_sleep_on_n2_plain.json`: `gpu_utilization_idle_percent` 45.5 and `baseline_device_MiB` 6,756 at start; its first 50 steps are about 10% slower than the profile pass (322 against 291 ms), so the reported 160.2 ms is slightly high. Output says "No other compute job ran during the timing sweeps. Utilization sampled just before each run was 1 to 8%": reported 1 to 8%, raw 0.2 to 45.5% over the timing runs. Fix: rerun `envs_full_sleep_on_n1_profile` and `envs_full_sleep_on_n2_plain` on an idle GPU, and make the script refuse to start when the baseline is not idle.
2. **Wrong bullet in P2.** "On the streaming modes [sleeping] changes the step time by less than 2 ms at every count": reported < 2 ms, raw 204.8 ms (off) against 198.8 ms (on) = 6.0 ms, files `envs_stream_cache_sleep_{off,on}_n32_plain.json`. It holds per environment (0.19 ms). Fix the sentence.
3. **Two of the six out-of-memory failures are not shown to be memory limits.** `envs_stream_cache_sleep_{off,on}_n64_plain.json` fail in `create_env` with "Failed to allocate 97168896 bytes" while the monitor's peak is 4,806 / 4,902 MiB for the process and 6,144 / 6,241 MiB for the device (of 32,607). The other four failures peak at 29.7 to 30.6 GB. Either the 0.25 s monitor missed a fast allocation, or the failure is something else. The projected need (64 x 749 MiB = 48 GB) makes OOM plausible, but it is not demonstrated. Also, counts were doubled, so "largest count that fits" is only known to be between 32 and 63 (4 and 7, 2 and 3 for full). Fix: say so, and log the allocator's request total at failure.
4. **Physics time larger than step time in four rows, not explained.** Full, sleep on: 50.4 against 46.8 ms (25.6 m), 97.6 against 85.6 (51.2 m), 194.9 against 164.2 (102.4 m), and the contaminated 124.42 against 77.49 (1 env). 5. **Sleep-on means for `full` include the falling-asleep transient.** `envs_full_sleep_on_n1_plain`: 140 ms in the first 50 steps, 67 ms steady from step 200; the reported mean is 77.5 ms and the p95 of 116.2 ms is the transient. "Sleeping ... adds about 1.5 ms per step" for streaming: raw 0.4 to 1.6 ms (0.4 ms with cache at 3.2 m).

## Discrepancies (write-up value, recomputed value, source), most important first
1. **P3 headline "With the cache the revisited track matches full in height and in force".** Height: supported (0.96 / 1.06 cm rms against 0.83 / 0.91 cm for the repeat). Force: lap-2 mean along the motion -29.44 against -26.69 N, a gap of 2.75 N (10.3%), seven times the 0.38 N between the two full runs; lap 1 gap 5.16 N (8.1%) against 0.15 N. Two more pairs of nominally identical runs exist and confirm that the mean is repeatable to about 0.1 N: `settle/cache_template_unsettled2` against the main cache run (-58.85 / -58.74 N) and `settle/cache_template_unsettled` against `history_settled_template` (-91.15 / -91.21 N). So the 8 to 10% residual is a real difference, not noise. The write-up states the residual in its own bullet; the headline and the sentence "differences of this size are noise" should not be read as covering it. What the data supports: the cache reproduces the drop in drag on the revisited track (59 to 29 N; full 64 to 27 N) to within 10%, and without it there is no drop.
2. **Sleep on is described too mildly ("Forces agree less").** On lap 1 with sleep on, the cache is no closer to full than the mode without cache: mean -50.7 against -74.0 N (31% low; no cache -53.9 N, 27% low), rms 21.3 / 18.5 / 12.7 N against 20.4 / 14.9 / 11.3 N without cache. On lap 2 the cache is 17% below full (-26.5 against -32.1 N) with rms 7.2 / 7.5 / 5.6 N. There is no repeat of full with sleep on, so no noise level for these rows. Since the task presents sleeping as part of the method, the P3 force claim holds for sleep off only and the text should say so. Files `history_*_sleep_on.{json,npz}`.
3. **Sleeping counts in the cached pool.** Write-up: "3,000 to 8,000 particles". Raw (`history_stream_cache_sleep_on.npz`, `activity`, 59 samples during the laps): 129 to 14,159, median 3,501, quartiles 798 and 8,433. 4. **P1 bullet "Sleeping ... adds 1.0 to 1.6 ms per step and about 100 MiB" on the streaming modes.** Raw: 1.04 to 1.49 ms without cache, 0.06 to 1.76 ms with cache (0.06 at 3.2 m; 1.71, 1.62, 1.76 at 12.8, 25.6, 51.2 m). 6. **Residual bullet: "the repeat of full differs by under 1%".** Raw: 0.2% on lap 1, 1.4% on lap 2 (0.38 of 26.69 N).
