# T4: retired (merged into T3)

- **Status:** retired on 2026-11-18. See `DECISIONS.md` of that date.

T4 was "Sensitivity to material randomization and resolution (Q4)". It no longer exists as a task:

- **Material.** Robustness to soft materials is now axis B of [T3](T3.md). Policies trained on nominal-only or narrow material ranges are not trained.
- **Particle resolution.** Dropped.

`outputs/T4.md` is kept as a record of what was inspected before the change (current material randomization ranges, the particle and grid resolution in use, and the September resolution playback sweep). It is unchecked.

The number T4 is not reused, so references to T5, T6, and T7 stay valid.
