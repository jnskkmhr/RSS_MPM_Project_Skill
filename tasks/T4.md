## Goal
Find how much material randomization the policy needs, and the coarsest particle resolution that still gives a transferable policy.

## Steps
1. **Material range.** Train G1 velocity tracking with (a) nominal material only, (b) a narrow range, (c) a wide range. Evaluate on held-out material parameters.
2. **Hardware.** Compare the nominal and wide-range policies on the T3 test bed.
3. **Resolution.** Train at several particle spacings; evaluate every policy at the finest resolution; record simulation throughput at each spacing.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The three randomization ranges written out per material parameter, plus the held-out evaluation range.
2. Success rate and tracking errors for the three policies on held-out materials.
3. Hardware result for nominal vs. wide range, with trial counts.
4. Policy performance and throughput against particle spacing, in one table or plot.
5. A recommended range and spacing, with the evidence for each.
