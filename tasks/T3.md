## Goal
Show whether training on MPM gives better locomotion on real granular terrain than training on rigid terrain or the RFT contact model, and identify the gait differences that explain the result.

## Starting points
- Matched training recipe for Rigid-rough and RFT against moving-patch MPM: `osmo/osmo_contact_benchmark.md`
- Existing evaluation harness and trained policies (Rigid rough, RFT flat, RFT rough, MPM rough): `examples/RSS/locomotion/README.md`. Check whether these runs still match the current recipe before reusing them.
- RFT model: `contrib/soft_contact/`

## Policies
1. **Rigid:** rigid rough terrain in MuJoCo Warp with randomized friction and contact compliance
2. **RFT:** 3D RFT contact model
3. **MPM-Flat:** flat MPM terrain
4. **MPM-Rough:** rough MPM terrain

All share robot asset, observations, rewards, and training budget. List every intentional difference.

## Steps
1. Train the four policies.
2. Evaluate in MPM on held-out terrain shapes and material parameters (supporting evidence only).
3. Log gait metrics for all four policies.
4. Hardware: compare Rigid, RFT, and MPM-Rough over several commanded speeds, trials in randomized order, on at least one terrain; two conditions (for example loose and compacted) if possible.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Simulation table: success rate, velocity and yaw tracking error per policy, with episode count.
2. Gait metrics per policy: foot sinkage, foot slip, foot clearance, gait timing, pelvis acceleration, cost of transport.
3. Hardware table: success rate and velocity tracking per policy and commanded speed, with trials per cell and failures described.
4. A stated mechanism linking a gait difference to a baseline failure mode, backed by the metrics, or a statement that none was found.
5. If the baselines match MPM on hardware, report that plainly.
