## Goal
Show that policies trained on MPM succeed across different embodiments and different tasks.

Towed objects: a box first. A sphere, a cylinder, and shapes with several edges can be added to make towing harder.

One policy is trained per embodiment and task. Nothing is transferred between them. Hardware is not part of this task; it is in T3.

## Embodiments and tasks

| Embodiment | Task | Who |
|---|---|---|
| UR10 (manipulator) | Particle pushing: push granular material into a bin | Max, with `IsaacContrib-UR10-Particle-Push` from Isaac Lab `contrib` |
| Go2 (quadruped) | Walking | T2 session |
| G1 (humanoid) | Walking | T2 session |
| G1 (humanoid) | Object interaction: towing an object while tracking a torso velocity command (`vx`, `vy`, `wz`) | T2 session |

## Steps
1. **Train** a policy for each row of the table: UR10 particle pushing, Go2 walking, G1 walking, and G1 object towing. Training is plain PPO.
2. **Evaluate** each policy with a fixed number of episodes and seeds, and report the metrics below.

Max hands over the UR10 results with their provenance (commit, run ID, command, episode count); the T2 session records them.

## Acceptance criteria

| # | Question | Metrics | How it is measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Does MPM training succeed for different embodiments? | For UR10, Go2, and G1: success rate and the reward curve over training | Steps 1, 2 |
| P2 | Does it succeed for different tasks? | Task-specific metrics. Particle pushing: fraction of material delivered and fraction spilled. Walking: velocity tracking error, yaw tracking error, travel distance. Object towing: torso velocity tracking error (`vx`, `vy`) and yaw rate tracking error (`wz`), goal-reaching success rate (the velocity command is applied until the robot reaches a goal), and robot fall rate, per towed object | Steps 1, 2 |

Measurement rules:
1. **Success rate and task metrics come from the evaluation** in step 2, with the episode count stated. The reward curve comes from the training log.
2. **Conditions** for every result: task ID, commit, run ID, checkpoint, seeds, `num_envs`, GPU, and training wall-clock time.
3. **One snapshot figure** per embodiment and task.
