## Goal
Show that the framework trains working policies for a manipulator and a humanoid, on manipulation, locomotion, and loco-manipulation, in practical training time, and that the manipulation and humanoid policies run on hardware.

## Starting points
- G1 velocity tracking: moving-patch tasks under `contrib/velocity_mpm/`
- G1 box pushing: `IsaacContrib-Push-G1-29dof-MPM-ObjectPush` (plus separate locomotion and manipulation tasks under `contrib/push/`)
- Manipulator: `IsaacContrib-UR10-Particle-Push` exists; the Franka task does not
- Quadruped: undecided (see `DECISIONS.md`)

## Steps
1. Implement or port the Franka particle-pushing task.
2. Train all three tasks; record training wall-clock time, `num_envs`, and GPU.
3. Evaluate in simulation with the metrics below.
4. Deploy Franka pushing and G1 box pushing on hardware (box pose from motion capture).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Franka: fraction of material delivered to the target region and fraction spilled, over a stated number of episodes.
2. G1 velocity tracking: success rate, velocity tracking error, yaw tracking error.
3. G1 box pushing: the above plus box velocity tracking and box tipping rate.
4. Training wall-clock time per task.
5. Hardware: the same task metrics over a stated number of trials, with failures counted.
6. One snapshot figure per task.
